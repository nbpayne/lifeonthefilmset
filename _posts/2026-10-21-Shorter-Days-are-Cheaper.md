  Ben Allan is a renowned, Australian cinematographer as well as being an 
  "accidental" producer. During COVID he produced and shot the film "Dark Noise"

  shooting to eight hour days. When he redid his schedule and budget around 
  these shorter days he unexpectedly found that the change was saving money. Ben
  talks about this change and the other performance benefits he experienced of 
  working with a happy, well rested crew.
